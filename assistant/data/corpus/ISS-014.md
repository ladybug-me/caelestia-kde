# ISS-014 — kde colour scheme auto sets to material you dark

State: CLOSED, stateReason COMPLETED · opened 2026-06-28 by 26heij.

## Problem (original report, condensed)

After running the setup.sh and prompting to keep my existing theme the colour scheme still changed to Material You Dark. I changed it to custom theme i use  in system settings and reran the setup.sh to check if it was an actual bug and  again the colour schema changed to material you dark. i changed it to my custom once again and did a **reboot and it changed again**.
I checked it with a logout and it aslo setted the colour scheme to material you dark.
Then i changed the colur scheme to material you Light and did a log out , login and again i was on material you Dark. then i tried with other material you colors and and other colors and they were also replaced by Material You Dark after a logout login.

- Basically everytime **quickshell is being reloaded** (logout/in , reboot, running setup.sh) the the color ik kde is seted to **Material You Dark** color automatically.

- Also for every setup.sh run, that many material you colors are being installed.
   3 run = 3 set of material you dark , light , dark(darker titlebar) , light(darker titlebar)
