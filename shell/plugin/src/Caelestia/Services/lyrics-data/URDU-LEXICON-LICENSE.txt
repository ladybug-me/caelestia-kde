Urdu romanization lexicon derived from the Dakshina dataset v1.0 (2020).
Source: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/dakshina
Data: https://storage.googleapis.com/gresearch/dakshina/dakshina_dataset_v1.0.tar
Authors: Brian Roark, Lawrence Wolf-Sonkin, Christo Kirov, Sabrina J. Mielke,
Cibu Johny, Isin Demirsahin, and Keith Hall.
Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.294/

Modification: for each of 25,000 Urdu words in ur.translit.sampled.train.tsv,
retain the most frequently attested Roman spelling, lowercase, as two TSV fields.
This derived data remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. The data license does
not change the license of the shell code. No song lyrics are included here.
